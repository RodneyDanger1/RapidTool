`token-ledger.ts`, `components/console.tsx`, `scripts/rapidtool-cli.mjs`). Hunt the **problem-causer
class** — the things that make a *plan* fail rather than a *test*:

- **hidden coupling**: an edit in file A silently requires a matching change in B (name both lines);

- **unbounded/heavy work**: paths a new lane's edits could make quadratic or memory-hungry on this 8 GB box;
- **docstring-vs-code drift** in the exact functions the plan names (the cheapest source of future wasted work).
